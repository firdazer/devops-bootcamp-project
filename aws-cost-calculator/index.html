About these estimates

All prices are on-demand list prices in USD, based on published AWS pricing at build time. Actual costs vary by usage, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, Free Tier eligibility and data transfer patterns. Always confirm final pricing on the AWS Pricing page.

Data transfer pricing reflects aggregate-free-tier rates. Free Tier is applied only where usage falls within the free allowances.
